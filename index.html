About me

Hi, I’m Michael.

I’m a hobbyist photographer based in Fife, Scotland. I enjoy capturing natural moments, trying different styles and improving my skills through personal projects.

This site is a place to share my favourite work and complete galleries. I’m particularly interested in portraits, family moments, automotive photography, nature and local events.
